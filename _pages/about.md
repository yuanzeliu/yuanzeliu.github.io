    <h1>Collective psychology and cultural evolution</h1>
  </div>

  <div class="home-hero__body">
    <p class="home-hero__lede">
      I study collective psychology and cultural evolution: how interactions among individuals give rise to collective patterns of thought and behavior, how these patterns change over time, and how they in turn shape individual psychology. I am especially interested in how these dynamics respond to changes in social ecology, including new institutions and technologies such as large AI models.
    </p>

<section class="home-section home-section--compact">
  <div class="home-section__intro">
    <div>
      <span class="section-kicker">Research agenda</span>
      <h2>My research is organized around three questions.</h2>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="research-pillars">
    <details class="research-pillar">
      <summary>
        <span class="research-pillar__number">01</span>
        <h3>How do psychology and culture adapt to changing social environments?</h3>
        <span class="research-pillar__abstract">I examine how perception, judgment, and intergroup attitudes emerge from and co-evolve with social ecology.</span>
        <span class="research-pillar__toggle">Research focus</span>
      </summary>
      <div class="research-pillar__statement">
        <p>I study how psychological processes such as person perception, social judgment, and intergroup attitudes emerge from and co-evolve with social ecology. I am particularly interested in large-scale transformations such as state centralization, globalization, and digitalization, and in how they reshape the ways people think about and relate to one another.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="research-pillar">
      <summary>
        <span class="research-pillar__number">02</span>
        <h3>When do collective and cultural dynamics produce undesirable outcomes?</h3>
        <span class="research-pillar__abstract">I study mismatches between adaptive processes and changing environments, institutions, and evaluative standards.</span>
        <span class="research-pillar__toggle">Research focus</span>
      </summary>
      <div class="research-pillar__statement">
        <p>Processes that are adaptive in one environment, or under one criterion of success, may become maladaptive as environments and evaluative standards change. I study the origins and dynamics of such mismatches, including persistent prejudice and inequality, political polarization, and barriers to cross-cultural communication.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details class="research-pillar">
      <summary>
        <span class="research-pillar__number">03</span>
        <h3>How can people deliberately shape collective and cultural change?</h3>
        <span class="research-pillar__abstract">I investigate how institutions and technologies can reshape cultural transmission, coordination, and collective decision-making.</span>
        <span class="research-pillar__toggle">Research focus</span>
      </summary>
      <div class="research-pillar__statement">
        <p>I study how human collectives can steer their own development—for example, by changing which practices are adopted, which ideas spread, how information circulates, and how disagreements are managed. I am especially interested in how new institutions and technologies can alter these processes and potentially improve collective outcomes.</p>
      </div>
    </details>
  </div>
</section>
